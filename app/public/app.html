PMC Phone

PMC Phone for Android

The same phone as this website, as an app: calls ring when your phone is locked or the app is closed, you can hear them on the earpiece, a headset or the speaker, and an emergency call rings even on silent.

Download the app

Install it

  1. Tap Download the app, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open PMC Phone and sign in with your Windows name and password, the ones you use on a hospital PC.
  4. Allow the microphone and notifications. When it asks, let it run without battery limits, show calls on the lock screen, and allow Do Not Disturb access (only emergency calls use it).

Made by PMO ICT for Premiere Medical Center. The app opens only phone.pmcne.ph.
